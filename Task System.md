## Daily Rule

Pick three must-win tasks. Everything else is extra.

## Progress Rule

Every day, fill the `Daily Progress Input` fields with real numbers. They do not need to be impressive; they only need to be honest.

## Sections

- `Today's Anchor` keeps the day focused.
- `Must-Win Three` decides what matters most.
- `Time Blocks` gives the day shape.
- `Core Daily Checklist` tracks repeat priorities.
- `Quick Capture` catches loose thoughts.
- `Waiting For` tracks things blocked by other people.
- `Carry Forward` stops unfinished tasks from disappearing.
- `Progress Dashboard` reconnects the daily notes so progress is visible over time.

## Weekly Upgrade Idea

Create a weekly review later with:

- What worked.
- What slipped.
- What to repeat.
- What to remove.
